SITE: 10.0 x 10.0 m lot (100 m²), one street façade, party walls (mitoyen) on the other three sides: NO window, door or vent on party walls. Assume the street is 10-12 m wide (lotissement). Typical zone: "habitat économique / E2, immeubles R+2 sur cour".
HEIGHT: R+2 = ground + 2 floors, max 11.50 m total "toute superstructure confondue" (some communes 12 m). Use floor-to-floor 3.00 m on every level (2.80 m clear ceiling + 0.20 slab); ground floor may be 3.20. Roof: accessible terrace, acrotère (parapet) 1.10-1.20 m, stair bulkhead max 2.50 m above the terrace slab; many RAs require the bulkhead to be set back 3.0 m from the street façade (so either keep the stair cage ≥3 m from the façade, or state clearly that the design relies on the commune allowing a bulkhead at the façade).
FOOTPRINT / PATIO (the decisive rule): built footprint max 84% of the lot (CUS 84% in Casablanca E2). A courtyard ("patio"/"cour") open to the sky through all levels is compulsory: minimum 16 m² with a minimum "vue directe" (clear side) of 4.0 m, measured excluding any projection. Every HABITABLE room (salon, séjour, bedroom, kitchen) must take light and air either from the street façade or from this courtyard. A smaller "courette" may only serve service rooms (bathroom, WC, laundry) and is optional. The patio can sit against the rear or a side party wall or be internal; position is free, area and 4 m side are not. On the ground floor the patio ground surface can be given to the ground-floor apartment as a private "cour" (a selling point), it still lights the floors above.
WINDOWS: each habitable room needs glazed openings ≥ 1/10 of its floor area and never < 1 m². Bathrooms/WC need a window on the patio or courette, or a ventilation duct (show a `tech` box for the duct if used).
ROOMS (minimums by decree; targets for sellability in brackets): main room (salon) ≥ 12 m² net [target 16-19]; other bedrooms ≥ 9 m² net [master 11-13, others ≥ 9.5]; kitchen ≥ 5 m² [target 7-9, closed or semi-open]; salle de bain ≥ 1.30 m² [target 3.5-4.5 with Italian shower]; a separate guest WC [≥ 1.2 m²]; minimum clear width of any habitable room 2.35 m [target ≥ 2.8]. Every apartment: 3 bedrooms, salon marocain (U of sedari 0.70 deep along 3 walls), a place for a dining table (hall/séjour or inside the salon), storage (placards/débarras), a balcony on the street side of 0.80-1.00 m projection (allowed only if the street is ≥ 10-12 m wide; ≥ 1.0 m away from each party line; none at ground level). Ground floor windows on the street get grilles/anti-effraction glazing (note only).
STAIR: shared cage, U-shaped two flights, clear flight width ≥ 1.00 m (Casablanca AMP; 1.10 preferred), 17 risers of 17.6 cm for 3.00 m, treads ≥ 27 cm, landings ≥ 1.00 m, the cage continues to the roof. Expect a cage of about 2.40-2.50 m x 4.20-4.60 m. Ground floor: street door ≥ 1.20 m, an entrance hall/corridor ≥ 1.20 m wide from the street to the stair, a meter cupboard ("compteurs", `tech`, ≥ 0.6 x 1.2 m) near the street door. One apartment door per landing.
STRUCTURE / MEP: reinforced-concrete frame (RPS 2011 zone 2-4), columns ≥ 25 x 25 cm on a regular grid ≤ 5.0 m; keep wet rooms (kitchen, bath, WC) stacked vertically and grouped on one or two plumbing stacks against the patio or a duct; gas water heater point in kitchen or bath; laundry on the roof.
ROOF: terrace slab with: stair bulkhead, one buanderie per apartment (about 2 x 3 m each) or one shared 6 m² buanderie + 3 storage boxes, a zone for water tanks / solar water heaters (`tech`), the rest open terrace (the top-floor apartment may get exclusive use of part of it as a sales premium). Parking: assume waived for lots ≤ 160 m² in an authorised lotissement (verify); do NOT put a garage in the plan.
NON-NEGOTIABLE OUTPUT RULES: room boxes tile the 10 x 10 exactly (checker must pass), 0.1 m grid, balcony is the only box outside the plot, no window on party walls, every habitable room lit from street or patio, patio ≥ 16 m² with ≥ 4.0 m min side.
